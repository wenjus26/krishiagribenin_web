Strategic Hub: Glo-Djigbé (GDIZ)

Our partnership with the Glo-Djigbé Industrial Zone (GDIZ) is the cornerstone of our operational excellence. By integrating into this world-class industrial ecosystem, Krishi Agri Benin benefits from cutting-edge infrastructure, streamlined logistics, and a direct gateway to global markets.
